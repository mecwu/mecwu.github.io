# Lo-Ying (Mecole) Wu — Portfolio

Personal portfolio built with [Quarto](https://quarto.org), showcasing project work in data analytics, financial modeling, and sustainability/ESG analysis.

## About

M.S. in Applied Business Analytics candidate at Boston University, with prior experience in ESG consulting (Deloitte) and data analysis (Sustainable Business Network). This site collects academic and independent projects spanning statistical modeling, machine learning, and financial valuation.

## Featured Projects

- **Job Market Analysis: AI vs. Non-AI Salary Trends** — Compared Linear Regression and Random Forest models across 70,000+ job postings to identify key salary drivers; built an interactive salary forecasting tool.
- **Bayesian Risk Analysis: Pharmaceutical Production Strategy** — Monte Carlo and MCMC simulation to evaluate a $20M production-strategy investment decision under regulatory and demand uncertainty.
- **DCF Valuation: Coca-Cola Inc. (NYSE: KO)** — Independent 3-statement financial model built from SEC filings, with WACC, terminal value, and sensitivity analysis.
- **Brewery Business Strategy Simulation** — Market positioning, SWOT/PEST analysis, and go-to-market strategy for a Boston-area brewery concept.
